Challenges

As the editor of the team, I was met with many clips that are too short or with inconsistent lighting (different camera settings).

Solutions

What I did to not make the clips appear so short is to use a fade-in/out effect. This allowed to the transition between clips to be smooth. As for the lighting, I used an adjustment layer to apply a filter on all clips, when used curves to adjust the lighting myself.
