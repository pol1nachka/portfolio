Project 2: Interviews

The goal of this project was to create an interview with a person of your choice. I chose my sister since she is someone very talented at her young age. This interview is quick and simple. I loved playing around with b-roll.

My challenges were that my footage was often shaky and not stable, which is why I had to cut a lot of it out. Also, some of the interview is missing because my sister forgot to turn on her microphone again. And the audio was not clear at all. Despite that, I'm still satisfied with the result.
